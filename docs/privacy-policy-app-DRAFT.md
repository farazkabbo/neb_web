## The short version

- neb helps you learn. To do that, it has to see and hear some of your studying. **You decide when.**
- neb only looks at your screen **when you circle something or ask**. It does not record your screen in the background.
- Voice listening is **optional**. Silent mode gives you everything without a microphone.
- **Your notes are yours.** We don't sell your data, show you ads, or use your notebooks, voice or screen content to train AI models [confirm with every AI provider's terms].
- You can export or delete everything, any time.

## 1. Who we are

neb ("neb", "we", "us") provides the neb learning app, the neb website (myneb.si) and related services (together, the "Service"). We are the controller of your personal information, except where we act for a school (see section 11).

### 2.2 Your learning content (the heart of neb)
| Content | When it's collected | What it's used for |
|---|---|---|

| **Course materials** (syllabi, textbooks, lecture PDFs you upload) | When you upload them | Building your lessons and pointing explanations to your own pages |
| **Learning progress** (what you did on your own, with a hint, or needs review; review schedule; exam dates) | As you study | Honest progress tracking, spaced review and exam planning |

## 3. How we use your information
1. **To provide the Service:** run the tutor, draw explanations, check your work, save notebooks, build course lessons and track progress.
2. **To decide when to help:** Mew evaluates voice and writing signals so neb speaks only when needed.
3. **To keep neb safe and working:** prevent abuse, fix bugs, secure accounts.
4. **To improve neb:** using aggregated and de-identified usage data, plus content you **explicitly choose to share** (for example a "report this explanation" button). We do **not** use your notebooks, voice, screen captures or uploaded materials to train AI models, ours or anyone else's, unless you opt in [confirm; remove the opt-in if there isn't one].
5. **To communicate:** account emails, important service notices and, with your consent, product news (you can unsubscribe anytime).
6. **To comply with law** and enforce our Terms.

We **never** sell your personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build advertising profiles.

## 4. AI processing
neb uses AI models to understand your work and generate explanations. Some processing is done by third-party AI providers acting on our behalf: [list, e.g. "OpenAI (US), Anthropic (US), Google (US/EU)"]. We send only what's needed for the request (for example your circled region and question, not your whole notebook), under contracts that **prohibit providers from training on your data** and limit retention to [0–30] days for abuse monitoring [confirm per provider].

## 9. Your controls and rights
**In the app:**
- turn the microphone off, or use **Silent mode**
- choose which apps and windows neb may see when you circle; revoke screen permission in your OS settings at any time
- export your notebooks (PDF/images) and your data (machine-readable)
- delete individual notebooks, uploads, or your whole account

**Your rights** (depending on where you live): access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawing consent, and not being discriminated against for using these rights. Email **mynebsi@gmail.com** and we'll reply within 30 days (45 for some US states).

- **EU / EEA / UK:** you can complain to your data-protection authority (in Slovenia: the Information Commissioner).
- **Canada:** neb complies with PIPEDA and applicable provincial laws (e.g. Québec Law 25). You can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
- **United States:** residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), New York and other states have the rights above. We don't sell or share personal information, and we honour Global Privacy Control.

## 10. Children and teens
- neb is designed for students, including teens.
- **Under 13:** a parent, guardian or school must create or approve the account (COPPA). We collect only what's needed for learning and never advertise to children. Parents can review, export or delete their child's data by emailing us.
- **Under 16 in the EU/UK** (or the local age of digital consent): we need a parent's or guardian's consent.
- Teen accounts get the most protective settings by default: no marketing emails without consent, and no public sharing.

## 11. Schools and teachers
If a school provides neb, the school controls student data and we act as its processor/service provider, under a written agreement that complies with laws such as **FERPA** (US) and state student-privacy laws. We use student data only to provide the Service to the school, never for advertising, and delete it when the school asks or the agreement ends.

## 12. Accessibility
Silent mode lets students who are non-verbal, or studying where they can't speak, use every feature by typing or boxing written questions. Choosing Silent mode means no audio is collected.

## 13. Security
- encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
- least-privilege access for staff, with access logged
- regular security reviews
- server-only database access

We will notify you and the relevant authorities of a data breach as required by law.

We'll update the effective date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
